## 7. Content Recommendation & Gaps

### Content to Retain
- **Core Value Propositions**: "Profesionalisme & Integritas", "Teknologi & Inovasi", and "Komitmen pada Kepuasan Pelanggan".
- **Visi & Misi**: The vision statement and 4-point mission list are sound and form the core identity.
- **Services Taxonomy**: The categories (Asuransi Kumpulan, Asuransi Umum, Bancassurance) and their respective list items are complete and should be kept.

### Content to Rewrite or Update
- **"Asuransi Kejiwaan"**: Must be renamed to **"Asuransi Jiwa"** (Life Insurance) to maintain professional credibility.
- **Contact Details**: Replace placeholder phone `0812-3456-7890` with real phone details, and clarify the "388" office unit code.
- **Bancassurance Description**: Can be polished to sound more corporate and modern.
- **Dynamic Translation**: Next.js implementation should use a static, high-performance translation approach (e.g., file-based i18n dictionary files) rather than client-side widgets, which harm SEO and page load performance.

### Missing Content / Information Gaps
1. **Real Business Phone & Office Unit**: Need precise details to replace placeholders.
2. **Social Media Profiles**: No social links (LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp Business) are present on the current site.
3. **Interactive Contact Form**: Current "Konsultasi Sekarang" buttons simply redirect to a contacts info page. A modern, functional inquiry form is missing.
4. **Individual Partner Details**: A clear list of partner insurance companies as text elements rather than merged image files.
5. **Team / Leadership Profile**: Information about the company’s management or key consultants is completely absent.
6. **Business Credentials**: Certifications, registration numbers, or licensing info (e.g., OJK/AAUI certification details, if applicable).
